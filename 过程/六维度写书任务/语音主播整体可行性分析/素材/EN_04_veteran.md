## Notes
- ASMR's origin story: 2007-2009 whisper community → 2010 term "ASMR" coined → 2011 GentleWhispering launches → 2013 WhispersRed → 2016 Gibi (mainstream breakthrough)
- First-generation ASMRtists (2009) are uniformly retired; second-generation (2011-2013) are largely still active
- Male ASMR voice artists are a small but consistent niche (Zarbondb, FredsVoice, ASMR Muzz, DrV-ASMR, Ephemeral Rift)
- Veteran ASMRtists who survived: GentleWhispering (14 yrs), FredsVoice (14 yrs), WhispersRed (12 yrs), Gibi (10 yrs)
- Career longevity markers: diversified income (Patreon, sponsorships, merch), community-building, avoiding controversy
- The voice-actor-to-ASMR pipeline is a recurring career path (FredsVoice, ASMR Muzz, DrV-ASMR, Alekirser)
- Ephemeral Rift represents a cautionary case: veteran creator whose controversial behavior disrupted an otherwise long-running channel
- YouTube is the dominant platform for veteran ASMR creators; Twitch is secondary for most
