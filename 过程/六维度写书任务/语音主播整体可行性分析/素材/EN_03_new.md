## Notes
- 2026 debut wave is heavily VTuber-modeled — most new ASMR streamers use animated avatars rather than being pure audio
- E-ROI agency's "Rift" generation (March 2026) and Nyumeta agency (June 2026) represent institutional investment in voice/ASMR VTubers
- Rapid growth trajectories: Alekirser went from nothing to 172K YT + 184K TikTok in approximately one year
- Newcomer ASMR streamers average 50-250 concurrent viewers on Twitch — small but growing
- Zephyr UwU's "Zephyathon" format (daily streaming for launch) is a common debut strategy
- Most new voice streamers are female; male newcomers are notably absent from search results
- The VTuber model provides a bridge: voice-centered performance with optional visual layer — this may be the most realistic "voice streamer" archetype for discoverability
- Pure audio-only streamers (no video, no avatar) remain extremely hard to discover via search
