## Notes
- Vocal cord nodules are a recognized occupational hazard across voice-based professions — VTubers, ASMRtists, voice actors, singers
- Treatment path: vocal rest → speech therapy → surgery (if severe) → 3-6 month recovery
- Several Hololive VTubers have dealt with vocal cord nodules (Suisei, La+, Pekora, Subaru, Iroha, Raora)
- ASMR burnout appears driven by: algorithm changes, mental health strain, loss of passion, platform moderation (YouTube), and the tension between ASMR as relaxation art vs. content grind
- YouTube's demonetization/shadowbanning of ASMR channels is a recurring issue driving creators to Patreon
- Several ASMRtists deleted entire catalogs rather than leave them on platform (Olivia Kissper, others)
- The "hibernation" pattern (Pelageya ASMR) suggests a sub-phenomenon of intermittent burnout/return cycles
