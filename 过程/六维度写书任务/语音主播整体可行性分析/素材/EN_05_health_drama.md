## Notes
- **Vocal cord health crisis is a central theme** — nodules, polyps, and surgery are recurrent across VTubers, voice actors, and synth creators
- VTuber/vocal cord pipeline: long streaming hours + singing → vocal cord nodules → surgery → 3-6 month hiatus → possible recurrence
- Hololive alone: Suisei, La+, Pekora, Subaru, Iroha, Raora have all dealt with vocal cord issues
- **ASMR community drama pattern**: power abuse (Maimy), ideological extremism (Ephemeral Rift), platform vs. creator conflict (Krissy, CalmingCornerASMR)
- **Burnout is distinct from drama**: Sophie Michelle's case is pure passion-burnout — not scandal-driven, just emotional depletion
- YouTube's relationship with ASMR creators is increasingly adversarial: algorithm suppression, demonetization, channel termination
- The dual vulnerability of voice creators: biological (vocal cords are fragile, non-replaceable instruments) and platform (reliant on opaque algorithms)
- Ephemeral Rift is a rare case of a male ASMR veteran whose controversies are extensively documented — similar cases for female creators may exist but surface differently in search
- Meloco's "miracle" recovery and Raora's successful return show that surgical intervention + proper rest CAN restore vocal function — but recurrence risk remains
