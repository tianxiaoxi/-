## Notes
- ASMR is the dominant category for voice-only streaming on Twitch (ASMR ranked #32 overall, 10,111 avg viewers, 2,789 unique streamers)
- English-language ASMR is overwhelmingly female-voiced; male voice ASMR is a notable niche (FredsVoice, Tom Who)
- Top ASMR Twitch streamers average 800-1,500 concurrent viewers — modest compared to gaming streamers but with dedicated audiences
- Pure voice-only (no video at all) streamers are rare; most ASMR streamers use some visual element (even if just a static image or VTuber avatar)
- Discord and SUGO are emerging as platforms for pure voice-party rooms (not traditional streaming)
- Clubhouse-style apps (X Spaces, Discord Stage Channels) represent an adjacent but separate ecosystem
